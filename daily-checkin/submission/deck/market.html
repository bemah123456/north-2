Market opportunity

Start where risk is highest: the weeks after a hospital stay

~13M

US hospital stays a year by adults 65+ (AHRQ HCUP, 2012)

Who uses it

Older adults living at home, and the adult children who worry about them from a distance.

Who pays

Hospital-at-home programs, home-care agencies and Medicare Advantage plans, per patient per month. Families can also subscribe.

Why they’d pay: a missed delirium means a longer stay, a readmission or a move to a nursing home. Pricing and reimbursement are hypotheses we would test in a pilot.

Entry point: hand the app out at discharge, when families are paying attention and the risk is highest. A pilot with a local hospital-at-home team would test demand and price.
