How it works

The 4AT by voice, and one result for the family

4AT partWhat the app does
AlertnessNotices slow or missing replies
OrientationAge, birthday, place, year
AttentionMonths backwards, a rotating version, or repeating digits
Acute changeVoice vs. their normal, plus the family’s word

Results follow the 4AT bands: Looks like their usual self, Keep an eye on it, Call their doctor today.

Built: Expo app for iPhone, Android and web; Python server that transcribes, scores and sends reminders and family texts. Questions change order and wording daily so answers can’t be memorized. An optional evening check-in catches sundowning, and a 0–100 change score tracks the trend against their own normal. Switch to the live demo here if time allows.
