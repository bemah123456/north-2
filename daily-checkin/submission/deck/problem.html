What we’re trying to crack

Delirium is a sudden change in thinking, and at home it often goes unnoticed for days.

Up to 50%

of hospitalized seniors are affected

$164B

The quiet kind

The sleepy, withdrawn form is most common in older adults. It looks like a bad day.
