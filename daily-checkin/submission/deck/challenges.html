Key technical challenges

What made this hard, and how we handled it

ChallengeOur approach
No open dataset of delirious speechCompare each person with their own baseline; a small model trained on simulated “off” days until real data exists
Speech recognition mishears older voices8 models compared. On real speakers aged 60+, Whisper small with a month hint marked no right answers wrong in a quiet room (2% with noise). Unclear answers are asked again
A TV in the backgroundThe app listens to the room first and asks once if a TV is on. Tuned on real recordings, it asks on under 2% of quiet check-ins
Answers like “the year after the pandemic”Rules first; an AI judge only for unclear answers, and a person can override
False alarms erode trustDays off, gentle reminders before any text, and plain reasons with every result
Judges weight technical execution at 30%. Point at what runs today: 84 automated tests, recorded audio end to end, a room check and a re-ask for misheard answers. Questions also rotate daily so they can't be memorized.
