Impact

Days earlier, at home

  • Families call the doctor the day thinking changes, not days later
  • Seniors stay independent longer, with a 2-minute routine
  • Clinicians get a daily trend instead of a single snapshot

Sustainability

Better with every check-in

  • Each person’s baseline sharpens with use
  • Consented daily recordings become a dataset no one else has
  • Each senior brings relatives who see the value daily
  • Low cost to run: a phone and two minutes

Next

From demo to evidence

  • Screening, not diagnosis
  • Pilot with families after discharge
  • Compare with clinicians’ 4AT
  • Passive listening later, on a dedicated device
Owning the limits makes the vision believable: the voice model has only seen simulated data so far, and it hasn’t been tested against clinicians.
