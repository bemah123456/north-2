The heist plan

Two minutes a day, compared with their own normal

1

They talk

The app reads a few questions aloud and they answer in their own words. No typing, no menus.

2

We compare

Answers are scored with the 4AT, a validated delirium screen. Their voice is compared with their own usual days.

3

Family knows

One plain result with reasons. If they don’t open the app, the family gets a text, because skipping is a signal too.

The key idea: delirium is a change, so we compare each person with themselves, not with everyone else. Silence counts: withdrawal is how the quiet kind shows up.
